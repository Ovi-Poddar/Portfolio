About Me

I am a software engineer specializing in Generative AI, prompt engineering, and AI agent behavior optimization. My background spans full-stack engineering (.NET Core, Angular, PostgreSQL, AWS), AI/NLP research at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) — including an ESEM 2023 publication on code-review analytics — and enterprise LLM systems, where I focus on translating domain requirements into reliable agent behavior.

At IQVIA, I work on enterprise LLM-powered assistants in the life-sciences domain. My work sits at the intersection of prompt engineering, domain and business requirements, agent configuration, and output validation. I translate client feedback, business documentation, and available data semantics into system instructions and behavioral constraints, then iteratively evaluate and refine agent responses for accuracy, consistency, and adherence to domain requirements.

I work within LangChain/LangGraph-based agent systems, focusing on prompt configuration, behavior analysis, and evaluation — including LLM/agent observability with LangSmith — rather than backend agent implementation. In personal projects, I build agentic applications using LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, RAG, MCP, and multi-agent workflows. My broader interests lie at the intersection of LLMs, NLP, agentic systems, and software engineering, particularly in building reliable and domain-aware AI systems.
